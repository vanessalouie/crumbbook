About Crumb Book

Baking reels, turned into recipes you can follow.

What it does

Baking videos on Instagram are fun to watch but hard to bake from: the recipe is buried in a long caption full of emoji and hashtags. Crumb Book reads that caption and turns it into a tidy recipe card, with ingredients sorted into dry, wet, and mix-ins and the steps numbered in order. Your cards are saved in your own Recipe Box so you can find them again.

How to use it

  1. 📋

    1. Copy the link and caption

    On Instagram, copy the reel's link, then open the caption and copy its text.

  2. ✨

    2. Paste and extract

    Paste both on the home page and click Extract Recipe.

  3. 🗂️

    3. Save and organize

    Save the card, then search, filter, and star recipes in My Recipe Box.

💡 Why do I paste the caption myself?

Instagram doesn't let other websites read captions or comments automatically, so Crumb Book can't fetch them from the link. Copying the caption takes a few seconds, and it means the site works without needing your Instagram login.
